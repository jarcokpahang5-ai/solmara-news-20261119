Sum up 19 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $250: $50 for 1st place, $30 for 2nd, $15 for 3rd, $5 each for 4th to 10th and $3 each for 11th to 50th.

A picture-editor style infographic of Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn entirely in code (pure Python 3.13 standard library — no images downloaded, no third-party packages). It names the day and sums up its six biggest stories: the first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen and its joint peace declaration; the International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz as the US–Iran confrontation escalates; COP31 ministers racing to unblock the climate talks in Antalya; violence spreading across Bangladesh; and a migrant boat sinking off Senegal — plus briefs on the EU's proposed €45 billion loan to Ukraine and a record $190.4m Van Gogh sale.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output).
